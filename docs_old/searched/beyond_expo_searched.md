## 1. Business Model

BEYOND Expo is Asia's largest technology innovation and ecosystem expo (17 employees), organized by the Macau Technology General Association. Held annually at The Venetian Macao's Cotai Expo, the event showcases cutting-edge technology across consumer tech, healthcare, sustainability, AI, robotics, and startups. Revenue from exhibitor fees, sponsorship packages, delegate passes, media accreditation, investment matchmaking, and government/partnership funding.
