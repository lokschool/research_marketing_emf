## 1. Business Model

HKCEC Management Limited (140 employees) operates the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, one of Asia's premier convention and exhibition venues and an iconic Hong Kong waterfront landmark in Wanchai. The venue generates revenue from venue rental, exhibition space, conference facilities, banquet services, catering, and ancillary services. Managed as a professional venue operator.
