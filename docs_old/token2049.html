Pain Point Analysis

📋 Before Event — Planning & Sales

#1: Pain Point B1: Manual Sponsor & Exhibitor Sales

36 employees suggests manual sales team doing outreach, sponsor acquisition, and exhibitor management via traditional methods (email, calls, LinkedIn).
Impact: Cannot scale sponsor/exhibitor acquisition proportionally with attendee growth. Each new sponsor requires manual sales effort. No automated lead qualification or AI-driven matching.
Medium Urgency

#2: Pain Point B2: Geographic Concentration Risk

Only two locations (Singapore, Dubai). Crypto regulatory environments are volatile and could impact event viability in either jurisdiction.
Impact: Business model concentrated in two cities. Regulatory crackdown in Singapore or Dubai would devastate the business. Need geographic diversification and digital revenue streams.
Medium Urgency
🎯 During Event — Execution & Engagement

#3: Pain Point D1: Lean Team Running Massive Events — Scalability Crisis

36 employees running 20,000-attendee conferences with 800+ side events. Attendance doubled from 2023 (10K) to 2024 (20K). Heavy reliance on contractors and partners.
Impact: Team is stretched dangerously thin. Single point of failure risk is extreme. Cannot maintain quality while doubling in size with current headcount. Burnout risk for core team.
High Urgency

#4: Pain Point D2: No AI-Driven Attendee-Startup-Investor Matching

Crypto/Web3 audience is tech-savvy and expects digital-first experiences. Events have startups, investors, and developers — but no AI-powered matchmaking to connect them efficiently.
Impact: Attendees self-navigate networking. High-value connections missed. Event ROI for sponsors and attendees limited to serendipitous encounters rather than data-driven introductions.
Medium Urgency
📊 After Event — Follow-up & Data

#5: Pain Point A1: No Year-Round Community Platform

Flagship conferences are 2-3 days annually. No evidence of year-round digital engagement, community platform, or content hub between events. Crypto community is 24/7 online but TOKEN2049 only engages them twice a year.
Impact: Massive community building opportunity lost. Attendees and sponsors only get value during event days. Competitors (Consensus, ETHDenver) are building year-round digital communities.
High Urgency
