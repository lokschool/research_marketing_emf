## 2. Pain Point Analysis

### Pain Point 1: Micro Team Managing Capital-Intensive Operations
**Evidence:** 13 employees managing marquees, AV, staging, power distribution, catering equipment, ticketing, WiFi/CCTV, security — a full outdoor event infrastructure business.
**Impact:** 13 people cannot effectively manage inventory, logistics, client acquisition, event execution, and equipment maintenance simultaneously. Risk of operational failures at live events. Cannot scale to multiple simultaneous events.
**Urgency:** High — operational capacity stretched.

### Pain Point 2: Fully Manual Sales & Client Acquisition
**Evidence:** No evidence of digital marketing, automated outreach, or CRM. Likely reliant on word-of-mouth and personal networks for a niche HK outdoor event market.
**Impact:** Limited pipeline visibility. Cannot proactively target festival organizers, corporate event planners, or expand beyond existing client base. Revenue entirely dependent on existing relationships.
**Urgency:** High — growth constrained by manual sales.

### Pain Point 3: No Digital Event Management Platform
**Evidence:** Offers event WiFi, CCTV, ticketing, and payment systems — but no evidence of integrated event management software, real-time inventory management, or client portal.
**Impact:** Clients cannot self-serve (check availability, book equipment, track orders). Manual coordination increases error risk. Competitors with digital platforms offer superior client experience.
**Urgency:** Medium — competitive disadvantage.

### Pain Point 4: Equipment Rental Model — Capital Intensive, Low Margins
**Evidence:** Heavy equipment inventory (marquees, staging, AV, power, catering). All revenue from physical asset rental.
**Impact:** High capital expenditure for equipment purchase and maintenance. Low utilization during off-peak seasons. No digital/software revenue to smooth cash flow. Vulnerable to event seasonality.
**Urgency:** Medium — business model risk.
