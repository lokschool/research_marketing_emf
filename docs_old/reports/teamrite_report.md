**Impact:** May be trapped in "lifestyle business" mode — comfortable but not scaling. Risk of founder-dependency — business may not survive owner transition. Missed growth opportunities over two decades.
**Urgency:** Medium — long-term sustainability risk.

### Pain Point 2: Fully Manual Creative Services — No Automation
**Evidence:** Custom fabrication, large-scale floral displays, bespoke event design — all inherently manual, labor-intensive services. No evidence of digital tools for workflow, client management, or project delivery.
**Impact:** Revenue directly tied to labor hours. Cannot scale without proportional headcount increase. Each project requires full manual effort from design through execution.
**Urgency:** Medium — scalability constraint.

### Pain Point 3: No Digital Marketing or Lead Generation
**Evidence:** 14 employees with no evidence of digital marketing, automated outreach, or CRM-driven sales. 20+ year history suggests reliance on reputation and referrals.
**Impact:** Client pipeline dependent on personal networks. Cannot proactively target new sectors or expand beyond Hong Kong. Vulnerable if key client relationships change.
**Urgency:** High — growth ceiling from manual BD.

### Pain Point 4: Competition from Larger, Tech-Enabled Agencies
**Evidence:** Competitors like Eventist (66 employees) and Filament (14, but tech-forward with drones/AI) offer more scale or innovation. Teamrite's digital interaction design is forward-looking but the core business remains traditional.
**Impact:** Risk of losing bids to agencies with more modern capabilities, larger teams, or better digital engagement offerings.
**Urgency:** Medium — competitive pressure.
