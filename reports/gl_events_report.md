# GL events — Report

**Fit: Very High (9/10).** Enterprise-scale prospect. WeExpand's strongest potential client.

## Pain Points

1. **5,000 employees, massive manual sales force** — Each of 300+ trade shows and 50+ venues requires dedicated sales teams. AI automation offers 8-figure margin improvement potential. **Urgency: High.**
2. **Fragmented tech across acquisitions** — Growth through M&A creates siloed data, incompatible CRMs, and no unified attendee view. WeExpand can be the integration layer. **Urgency: High.**
3. **300+ trade shows operating independently** — Zero cross-show attendee data sharing. Massive cross-sell opportunity untapped. **Urgency: High.**
4. **Venue division is passive landlord** — 50+ venues offer space only. No digital services, lead capture, or attendee engagement platforms. WeExpand transforms venues into smart spaces. **Urgency: Medium.**
5. **Public company margin pressure** — Listed on Euronext. Shareholders demand efficiency gains. AI = demonstrable margin story. **Urgency: Medium.**
6. **Global scale requires localized outreach** — Operating in 20+ countries. Hyper-localized AI content and multi-language automated outreach critical. **Urgency: High.**

## Solution Fit

| Pain Point | WeExpand | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| 5,000 employees/manual sales | Autonomous workforce | **Strong** |
| Fragmented M&A tech | Unified CRM + AI platform | **Strong** |
| Siloed 300+ events | Cross-event data + community | **Strong** |
| Passive venues | Smart venue digital layer | **Strong** |
