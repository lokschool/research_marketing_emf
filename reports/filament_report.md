## 2. Pain Point Analysis

### Pain Point 1: Boutique Agency — Cannot Scale Premium Services
**Evidence:** 14 employees offering high-touch services: drone shows, AI event tech, luxury staging, VIP events. Each project is bespoke and labor-intensive.
**Impact:** Revenue ceiling directly tied to billable hours. Cannot take on multiple large projects simultaneously. Growth requires hiring equally talented creatives — hard to find and retain.
**Urgency:** High — growth constrained by talent availability.

### Pain Point 2: No Automated Client Acquisition
**Evidence:** Premium positioning suggests high-touch sales through networks and referrals. No evidence of digital marketing, automated outreach, or systematic lead generation.
**Impact:** Client pipeline is unpredictable and relationship-dependent. Cannot scale into new markets (Macau, Greater China, SE Asia) without boots on the ground — or AI-driven outreach.
**Urgency:** Medium — geographic expansion blocked.

### Pain Point 3: Technology Leadership Not Monetized as Platform
**Evidence:** Offers drone light shows and AI-powered event tech — rare capabilities for a 14-person agency. But these are sold as project services, not as a scalable platform or recurring revenue product.
**Impact:** Each drone show and AI deployment is a one-off project. Missed opportunity to productize these capabilities into SaaS or recurring service models.
**Urgency:** Medium — monetization gap.

### Pain Point 4: Heavy Production Dependency — Operational Risk
**Evidence:** Live events, drone shows, outdoor spectacles all carry significant operational risk. Small team means single point of failure for critical roles.
**Impact:** One key person's absence could derail a major event. No redundancy in creative or technical leadership. Business continuity risk.
**Urgency:** Medium — operational vulnerability.
