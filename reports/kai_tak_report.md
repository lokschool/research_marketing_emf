## 2. Pain Point Analysis

### Pain Point 1: New Venue — No Established Client Acquisition Engine
**Evidence:** Opened March 2025. 160 employees. Must build event pipeline from zero — competing against HKCEC (30+ year relationships) and AWE (20 years). 90+ events in first year impressive but primarily driven by government support and novelty factor.
**Impact:** Without systematic lead generation and automated outreach, client acquisition costs are high and pipeline is unpredictable. 160 employees suggests significant manual sales effort.
**Urgency:** High — first-mover advantage window closing.

### Pain Point 2: No Digital Engagement Layer for Visitors
**Evidence:** No evidence of AI chatbot, smart wayfinding, personalized event recommendations, or post-event attendee engagement. New venue built with modern physical infrastructure but no smart venue digital capabilities.
**Impact:** Venue is a "dumb" physical space — no first-party attendee data, no direct relationship with visitors, no upsell opportunities beyond ticket sales. Data ownership stays with event organizers.
**Urgency:** High — uniquely positioned to build this from scratch.

### Pain Point 3: Single-Location Revenue Concentration
**Evidence:** 28-hectare single site at Kai Tak. All revenue tied to one physical asset.
**Impact:** Revenue entirely dependent on Hong Kong event market health. No digital revenue streams. Vulnerable to regional disruptions (COVID-level events would be catastrophic).
**Urgency:** Medium — diversification needed.

### Pain Point 4: Manual Event Sales & Marketing
**Evidence:** 160 employees with no evidence of AI-driven marketing or automated B2B lead generation. Targeting concert promoters, sports federations, and corporate event planners likely through traditional outreach.
**Impact:** High cost-per-acquisition for events. Cannot efficiently target global promoters. 7M visitors in Year 1 but no mechanism to capture and nurture that audience data.
**Urgency:** Medium — operational efficiency gap.

### Pain Point 5: No Post-Event Data Monetization
**Evidence:** 90+ events, 7M+ visitors — zero evidence of attendee data analytics, exhibitor/vendor lead capture, or post-event engagement platforms.
**Impact:** Massive data generated by events goes completely uncaptured. $30B venue investment with no data monetization layer. Missed recurring revenue from data services, audience insights, and sponsor analytics.
**Urgency:** Medium — significant missed revenue opportunity.
