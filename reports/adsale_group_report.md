## 2. Pain Point Analysis

### Pain Point 1: Traditional Trade Show Model Under Digital Threat
**Evidence:** 170 employees running large physical exhibitions (CHINAPLAS: 390K sqm, 320K visitors). B2B sourcing increasingly moving online (Alibaba, Global Sources). No evidence of year-round digital platform.
**Impact:** Physical exhibition model faces existential threat from digital B2B platforms. If buyers can source online, exhibition value proposition erodes.
**Urgency:** High — industry disruption risk.

### Pain Point 2: Annual Event Cycle — 11-Month Engagement Gap
**Evidence:** CHINAPLAS and other Adsale shows are annual or biennial. Exhibitors and visitors engage once per year, then go dormant.
**Impact:** Massive relationship value lost between events. No recurring revenue from digital community. Exhibitors get episodic ROI rather than year-round value.
**Urgency:** High — community monetization gap.

### Pain Point 3: No AI-Driven Exhibitor-Visitor Matching
**Evidence:** 320K visitors at CHINAPLAS but no evidence of AI-powered matchmaking, smart recommendations, or automated lead capture for exhibitors.
**Impact:** Visitors self-navigate massive exhibition halls. Exhibitors rely on booth traffic rather than data-driven lead generation. Event ROI limited to physical encounters.
**Urgency:** Medium — exhibitor value gap.

### Pain Point 4: Manual Sales Across Multiple Industry Verticals
**Evidence:** 170 employees managing events across plastics, textiles, printing, lighting, data centres, automotive — broad portfolio requiring manual B2B sales for each vertical.
**Impact:** Cannot efficiently scale into new industry sectors without proportional headcount increase.
**Urgency:** Medium — growth bottleneck.

### Pain Point 5: Chinese Manufacturing Export Dependency
**Evidence:** Core value proposition is connecting Chinese manufacturers with global buyers. Geopolitical tensions and supply chain shifts directly impact event viability.
**Impact:** Business model heavily exposed to China trade policy.
**Urgency:** Medium — geopolitical risk.
