## 1. Business Model

Informa Markets is the world's largest B2B exhibition organizer, a division of Informa PLC (FTSE 100, London Stock Exchange). With 3,500+ employees, Informa Markets runs 550+ B2B exhibitions annually across 20+ industry sectors including health & nutrition, beauty & aesthetics, jewellery, construction, maritime, aviation, fashion, pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, and infrastructure. Revenue exceeds $1B from exhibition space sales, sponsorship, delegate fees, digital services, and market intelligence. Major Hong Kong presence with jewellery, beauty, and maritime events.
