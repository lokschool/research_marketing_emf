## 1. Business Model

121 Group operates as a mining investment event organizer, running "121 Mining Investment" conferences globally. The core model is curated 1-2-1 (one-to-one) meetings between mining company executives and qualified institutional investors. Revenue comes from mining company participation fees and investor registration. Platform metrics: 645 mining companies, 2,914 qualified investors, 10,457 meetings facilitated, 18 meetings average per event.
