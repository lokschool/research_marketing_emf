## 1. Business Model

1000Meetings (17 employees, 1000meetings.com.sg) is a Singapore-based venue sourcing and meetings management platform. Centralized RFP platform connecting event organizers with hotels and venues across Asia Pacific destinations.


## 2. Target Customers

Corporate event planners, meeting organizers, and procurement teams seeking venues in Asia Pacific destinations. B2B focus on corporate meetings, conferences, and MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) events.
