Executive Summary

KEYS Venue is a small, 18-person event management company operating across two distinct luxury markets in France (Paris and Cannes). While they offer a broad portfolio of services, the company is severely constrained by a basic digital infrastructure (WordPress, Google Analytics) and a fragmented brand presence, creating significant operational bottlenecks and limiting growth. Our solution directly addresses their critical need for automation, unified client management, and data-driven decision-making, which is essential for scaling their multi-location operations. With a high urgency score (0.9) and strong strategic alignment (0.85), KEYS Venue represents a high-priority, high-fit sales opportunity.
