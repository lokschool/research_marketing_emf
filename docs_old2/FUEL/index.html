B2B Sales Intelligence Report: FUEL

Executive Summary

FUEL is a 170-person Irish event management and brand experience company that delivers some of Ireland's largest festivals and corporate events. Despite strong creative capabilities, the company operates with a surprisingly lean tech stack (Mailchimp and Webflow only), creating significant operational bottlenecks in lead qualification, customer data management, and sales scalability. With a composite sell score of 0.72, FUEL represents a strong opportunity for our solution, particularly given the high urgency (0.9) driven by their launch of a new hybrid event platform (VStage) and clear pain points in digital infrastructure. Our solution directly addresses their most critical needs: automating lead qualification, unifying fragmented customer data, and enabling scalable project management across diverse service lines.

Company Overview

**Who They Are:** FUEL is a full-service brand experience and event management company based in Ireland. They specialize in four core service areas: - Brand experience and activations - Employee experience and wellness programs - Content creation and production services - Large-scale festival delivery (including some of Ireland's largest festivals)

**Size & Structure:** 170 employees, indicating a mid-sized agency with significant operational capacity but limited digital infrastructure.
