CloserStill Media is a London-based B2B events powerhouse operating over 200 market-leading events across technology, healthcare, learning/HR, and transport/infrastructure sectors. Despite its scale and a recent $1.8 billion valuation, the company faces significant operational inefficiencies stemming from fragmented data systems, manual sales qualification processes, and minimal digital infrastructure—our analysis detected only Google Tag Manager in their tech stack. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by automating lead scoring, unifying CRM data across their portfolio, and enabling data-driven market entry, which could reduce cost-per-qualified-lead by 30-40% and accelerate event launch cycles by 6-8 weeks.
