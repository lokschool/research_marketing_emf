B2B Sales Intelligence Report: Pyjama

Executive Summary

Pyjama is a Hong Kong-based event management company with 13 employees, offering end-to-end services from corporate events to weddings. Despite a strong value proposition, the company operates with a fragmented tech stack (Wix, WordPress, Segment) and lacks CRM or marketing automation, creating significant operational inefficiencies. Our solution directly addresses their most critical pain points—inefficient lifecycle management, limited lead generation, and underutilized customer data—with a composite sell score of 0.61 indicating strong alignment. Immediate engagement is recommended, focusing on demonstrating how our platform can streamline their event workflows and unlock revenue growth.

Company Overview

  • **Company Name:** Pyjama
  • **Headquarters:** Hong Kong
  • **Founded:** Not disclosed
  • **Employee Count:** 13
  • **Core Services:** Full-service event management including corporate events, festivals, weddings, and intimate gatherings; catering and equipment rentals
  • **Market Position:** Small, broad-service provider in Hong Kong's event industry, competing with both niche specialists and larger integrated firms
  • **Digital Maturity:** Medium—uses basic web tools (Wix, WordPress) and analytics (Segment) but no CRM, marketing automation, or dedicated event management software

Pyjama positions itself as a one-stop solution for event creation, but its small team and manual processes limit scalability. The company serves diverse event types, from corporate to personal, suggesting a need for flexible yet efficient operations.
