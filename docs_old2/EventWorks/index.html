Executive Summary

EventWorks Rentals is a Charleston-based tent and event rental company with 290 employees, serving weddings and special events in a $50 billion market segment. The company faces critical operational bottlenecks due to a limited tech stack (Shopify + Google Tag Manager only), resulting in manual sales qualification, underutilized customer data, and inefficient inventory management. With a composite sell score of **0.76**, strong budget probability (0.9), and high urgency (0.9), EventWorks represents a high-priority sales opportunity for solutions that automate lead scoring, CRM integration, and inventory optimization.
