Executive Summary

Informa Markets, a trading division of Informa PLC with approximately 3,500 employees, operates in the $1.1 trillion global event management market. The company faces critical challenges in leveraging its existing digital tools—primarily Google Analytics and Segment—to unify fragmented customer data, automate sales qualification, and accelerate market entry. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by integrating behavioral data, automating lead scoring, and enabling cross-border negotiation efficiency, positioning us to deliver a 10-15% improvement in conversion rates and a 20% reduction in time-to-revenue for new verticals.

Company Overview

Informa Markets is a major player in the global events and exhibitions industry, operating as a trading division of Informa PLC. With a workforce of approximately 3,500 employees, the company organizes trade shows, exhibitions, and conferences across multiple verticals and geographies. While specific founding date and headquarters location are not publicly confirmed, the company's global footprint is substantial, spanning markets in Asia, Europe, the Americas, and the Middle East.

The company operates in a market valued at $1.1 trillion (2023), growing at 10-12% CAGR through 2030. Key industry trends include hybrid event models, sustainability focus, AI-driven personalization, and niche trade shows. Informa Markets' current tech stack—Google Analytics and Segment—suggests moderate digital maturity, but significant untapped potential exists to transform these tools into competitive advantages.
