Beacon Events is a mid-sized global event producer (130 employees) specializing in the mining and resources industries, operating across Hong Kong, London, Riyadh, Melbourne, North America, and Europe. The company faces critical operational bottlenecks in market entry, localization, sales qualification, and scaling—compounded by a lean team managing complex cross-border partnerships. With a composite sell score of 0.71, strong urgency (0.9), and high pain fit (0.69), Beacon Events represents a high-potential target for our solution, which directly addresses their most pressing inefficiencies in lead qualification, funnel automation, and operational scaling.
