ReThink HK is a premier sustainability event organizer in Hong Kong, operating in a rapidly growing $1.1 trillion global event management market. The company faces critical challenges: high costs in qualifying sponsors, a competitive advantage gap in a crowded sustainability space, and sales funnel bottlenecks that hinder repeat participation. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by automating lead qualification, providing data-driven market validation, and enabling personalized attendee engagement, which aligns perfectly with ReThink HK’s urgent need to scale efficiently and maintain market leadership.

Company Overview

ReThink HK is a Hong Kong-based B2B event organizer focused on sustainability, circular economy, and net-zero strategies. They host an annual conference and exhibition that brings together corporate leaders, government officials, and solution providers. While specific revenue figures are undisclosed, the company operates as a mid-sized player in the regional events sector, with a strong brand presence in the sustainability niche. Their market position is defined by their ability to curate high-level content and attract premium sponsors, but they face increasing competition from larger, more digitally mature event platforms.
