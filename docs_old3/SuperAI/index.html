Executive Summary

SuperAI is a small but ambitious event organizer (20 employees) operating in the high-growth AI conference space. While they have a strong brand and a major annual event, they face critical operational and revenue risks due to an over-reliance on a single conference, a fragmented tech stack, and manual processes. Our analysis reveals a **high urgency (0.9)** for a unified sales and marketing automation solution, as they are currently underleveraging event data, struggling with partner management, and risking operational strain. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by integrating their existing tools (HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo) to automate lead qualification, personalize content, and streamline partner onboarding, thereby reducing risk and unlocking scalable revenue growth.

Company Overview

  • **Company:** SuperAI
  • **Size:** 20 employees (small, lean team)
  • **Core Business:** Organizer of a major annual AI conference and related workshops (e.g., SuperAI 2024, ethics series).
  • **Market Position:** A niche player in the AI events space, focusing on "AI ethics and decentralized intelligence." They operate primarily out of Singapore but target a global audience of attendees, startups, and sponsors.
  • **Tech Stack:** They have invested in a modern but complex stack including HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, Google Tag Manager, Webflow, and automation tools (Make.com, n8n). However, this stack is **underutilized and poorly integrated**, creating data silos rather than a unified system.
