The Adsale Group, a Hong Kong-based trade media and exhibition organizer with 170 employees, faces critical operational inefficiencies that our solution directly addresses. Despite record attendance at its flagship Chinaplas event, the company struggles with fragmented buyer management, manual lead qualification, and slow market expansion into new verticals like energy storage.
