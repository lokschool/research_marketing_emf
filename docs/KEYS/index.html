Executive Summary

KEYS Venue is a small, 18-person event management company operating across two distinct luxury markets (Paris and Cannes). While they offer a broad portfolio of services, they are severely limited by a basic digital infrastructure (WordPress, Google Analytics) and lack any CRM or marketing automation. This creates significant operational bottlenecks and missed revenue opportunities. Our solution directly addresses their most urgent pain points—fragmented client management, inefficient cross-location coordination, and a lack of data-driven decision-making—making us a high-fit partner to help them scale and compete effectively.

Company Overview

KEYS Venue is a boutique event management company specializing in high-end venues and services across two of France's most prestigious regions: Paris & Ile-de-France and Cannes & Côte d’Azur. With a team of 18 employees, they manage a diverse portfolio of events including showrooms, galas, corporate seminars, culinary workshops, and immersive experiences. Despite their broad service offering, the company operates with a fragmented digital presence (two separate websites) and a very low digital maturity, relying on basic tools like WordPress, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager. This positions them as a traditional player in a market that is rapidly digitizing, creating a clear opportunity for a technology partner.
