Executive Summary

FUEL is a 170-person Irish event management and brand experience company delivering festivals, content production, and employee experience services. Despite strong market positioning in a $1.1 trillion industry growing at 10.3% CAGR, FUEL operates with a critically weak digital infrastructure—relying solely on Mailchimp and Webflow—creating severe bottlenecks in lead qualification, customer data management, and operational scalability. Our solution directly addresses these gaps with a composite sell score of 0.72, driven by high urgency (0.9) and strong pain fit (0.78), making FUEL a high-priority target for immediate engagement.

Company Overview

**FUEL** is an Ireland-based event services company with 170 employees, specializing in: - Brand experience and activations - Employee experience programs - Content creation and production services - Large-scale festival delivery (including some of Ireland's largest festivals) - Venue project management

The company operates in the global event management market, valued at $1.1 trillion in 2023 with a projected 10.3% CAGR through 2030. FUEL has recently launched VStage, a hybrid event platform, signaling an attempt to pivot toward digital offerings. However, their tech stack remains limited to Mailchimp (email marketing) and Webflow (website builder), with no CRM, marketing automation, or analytics tools detected.
