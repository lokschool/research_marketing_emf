Executive Summary

Filament Limited is a 14-person event production and venue transformation company based in Hong Kong, operating in a $1.1 trillion global event management market growing at 10.3% CAGR. Despite having foundational analytics tools (Amplitude, Google Tag Manager), the company lacks CRM and marketing automation capabilities, creating critical gaps in lead generation, client qualification, and operational scalability. Our solution directly addresses these pain points with a composite sell score of 0.73, driven by high urgency (0.9) and strong strategic alignment (0.85), making this a compelling sales opportunity for a targeted CRM and marketing automation platform.
