Executive Summary

EventWorks Rentals is a Charleston-based event rental company with 290 employees, serving the wedding and special events market. Despite a strong local reputation, the company operates with a limited digital infrastructure—relying on Shopify and Google Tag Manager without CRM, marketing automation, or integrated inventory management systems. This creates significant operational bottlenecks, missed revenue opportunities, and vulnerability to more digitally savvy competitors. Our solution directly addresses these gaps with a composite sell score of 0.76, high urgency (0.9), and strong budget probability (0.9), making EventWorks a high-priority target for immediate engagement.
