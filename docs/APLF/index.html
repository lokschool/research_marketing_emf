Executive Summary

APLF Limited is a niche B2B exhibition organizer with 22 employees, focused exclusively on leather trade fairs. Despite operating in a $1.1 trillion event management market growing at 10.3% CAGR, APLF faces significant digital transformation gaps that limit its ability to capture leads, personalize attendee experiences, and scale operations. Our solution directly addresses APLF's most urgent pain points—inefficient lead qualification, fragmented marketing automation, and underutilized event data—with a composite sell score of 0.71 indicating strong sales potential if urgency is leveraged quickly.

The company operates with a lean team of 22 employees, though its founding date and headquarters location are not publicly disclosed.
