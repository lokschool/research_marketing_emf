Company Overview

Business Model

AsiaWorld-Expo (AWE) is a major exhibition and convention venue operator in Hong Kong, located adjacent to Hong Kong International Airport in Chek Lap Kok. Managed by AsiaWorld-Expo Management Limited (110 employees), the venue generates revenue from venue rental, event services, F&B concessions, and ancillary services. Opened in 2005. AWE competes directly with HKCEC (Wanchai) and the new Kai Tak Sports Park.
