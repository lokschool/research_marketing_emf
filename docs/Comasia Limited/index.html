Executive Summary

ComAsia Limited is a Hong Kong-based trade exhibition organizer with 54 employees, operating across multiple consumer retail sectors to connect international buyers with Asian suppliers. The company faces significant operational inefficiencies due to a lack of digital tools, including no CRM, marketing automation, or martech systems, which creates bottlenecks in lead qualification, market localization, and scaling. Company Overview

ComAsia Limited is a professional trade exhibition organizer and event management company headquartered in Hong Kong. With a team of 54 employees, the company designs platforms that connect international buyers with suppliers, manufacturers, and exporters from Asia and globally across key consumer retail product sectors. Their mission is to drive global trade and foster sustainable growth through physical and digital event experiences.
