Executive Summary

EX-R International is a 19-person Hong Kong-based experience reengineering company that designs creative concepts and experiences for brand events and launches. Despite operating in a rapidly growing $11.4 billion market with 11.2% CAGR, the company faces critical operational bottlenecks due to manual workflows, a limited tech stack (Amplitude + Squarespace), and severe scaling constraints. Our solution directly addresses their most urgent pain points—slow market entry, sales funnel inefficiencies, and a competitive advantage gap—by providing automated prototyping, integrated CRM, and data-driven personalization tools that can reduce turnaround times by 20-30% and enable them to compete with larger, platform-enabled rivals.

Company Overview

**Who They Are:** EX-R International is an "Experience Reengineering" company headquartered in Hong Kong (18C, Genesis, 33-35 Wong Chuk Hang Road, Wong Chuk Hang). They specialize in creative concept development and experience design for brand events and product launches.

**What They Do:** The company offers end-to-end services spanning creative concept development, event production, talent management (celebrities/influencers), PR coordination, and logistics management for international clients.
