Company Overview

JEC is the premier global organizer of composites industry events, most notably **JEC World**, the largest annual trade show for composite materials. The company operates as a media and events platform serving manufacturers, suppliers, and end-users across aerospace, automotive, construction, and renewable energy sectors.

**Key Profile Data:** - **Core Business:** Trade shows, conferences, and digital media for the composites industry - **Flagship Event:** JEC World (annual, Paris-based) - **Market Position:** Dominant player in composites event space, but facing pressure from digital-native competitors - **Geographic Focus:** Strong European presence, expanding into Asia and North America - **Digital Maturity:** Moderate – has launched JEC Forum and virtual components but lacks integrated digital sales tools
