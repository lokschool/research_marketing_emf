B2B Sales Intelligence Report: RX Global

Executive Summary

RX Global is a leading face-to-face event organizer producing over 400 events across 43 sectors in 22 countries, with 3,300 employees. The company faces significant operational challenges including slow market entry validation, inefficient localization processes, high sales qualification costs, and scaling difficulties as it expands into new regions like the Middle East. Our solution directly addresses these pain points with a composite sell score of 0.84, driven by strong pain fit (0.82), high budget probability (0.9), and digital readiness (0.9), making RX Global a high-priority target for immediate engagement.

Company Overview

RX Global is a global expert in creating and delivering face-to-face events, both in-person and virtual. Key facts:

  • **Size:** 3,300 employees
  • **Event Portfolio:** 400+ events annually across 43 industry sectors
  • **Geographic Reach:** 22 countries worldwide
  • **Core Business:** B2B exhibitions, conferences, and trade shows
  • **Market Position:** Major player in the $1.1 trillion global event management market, competing with Informa and Clarion
  • **Digital Maturity:** Medium-high, with tools like Lead Booster, Priority Plus Profile, and a tech stack including Drupal, Salesforce, Segment, and n8n
  • **Strategic Focus:** Expanding into the Middle East and launching sustainable event initiatives
