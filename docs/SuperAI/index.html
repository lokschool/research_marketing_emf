SuperAI is a lean, 20-person organization that operates a high-profile international conference and workshops focused on AI ethics and decentralized intelligence. SuperAI is a small, specialized event organizer with a team of approximately 20 employees. Their flagship event, "SuperAI 2024," along with a series of workshops, positions them as a thought leader in a niche but rapidly growing market.
