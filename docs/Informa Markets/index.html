Executive Summary

Informa Markets, a trading division of Informa PLC with approximately 3,500 employees, operates in the $1.1 trillion global event management market. The company faces critical challenges in leveraging its existing digital tools (Google Analytics and Segment) for market validation, unifying fragmented customer data across geographies, and automating manual sales qualification processes. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by providing a unified customer data platform with predictive analytics and automated lead scoring, enabling faster market entry, reduced sales costs, and a sustainable competitive advantage.

Company Overview

Informa Markets is a major trading division of Informa PLC, a leading global events, digital services, and academic knowledge provider. With approximately 3,500 employees, the company operates across multiple geographies, organizing trade shows, exhibitions, and B2B events. The global event management market was valued at approximately $1.1 trillion in 2023, with exhibitions and trade shows accounting for a significant share. The market is projected to grow at a CAGR of 10-12% from 2024 to 2030, driven by digital transformation and hybrid event models. Informa Markets currently uses Google Analytics and Segment as its primary digital tools, but evidence suggests these are underleveraged for rapid market validation and customer insight generation.
