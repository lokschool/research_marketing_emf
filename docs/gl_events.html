Company Overview

Business Model

GL events is a French multinational event giant (5,000+ employees), publicly listed on Euronext Paris, with revenue ~€1.4B. Operates through three divisions: GL events Live (event services, equipment rental, stand construction), GL events Exhibitions (owns/manages 300+ B2B and B2C trade shows), and GL events Venues (operates 50+ venues globally including convention centres, arenas, and event spaces). Hong Kong subsidiary (16 employees) provides local exhibition services. Major events in Greater China through partnership/JV models.
