Executive Summary

SYMA, a Swiss specialist in modular exhibition systems, is facing a critical competitive gap. While they have a strong product focus on architectural clarity and technical precision, their reliance on a basic digital tech stack (Wix, Brevo, Google Analytics) is creating significant bottlenecks in lead generation, sales qualification, and market expansion. Our solution directly addresses these inefficiencies by providing the advanced CRM, automation, and data analytics capabilities they lack, enabling them to accelerate market entry, reduce sales costs, and scale operations internationally. With a composite sell score of 0.66 and high urgency around digital transformation, SYMA represents a strong, actionable opportunity for our platform.
