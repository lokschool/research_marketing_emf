Executive Summary

Pyjama is a Hong Kong-based event management company with 13 employees, offering end-to-end services from corporate events to weddings. Despite a broad service range, the company operates with a fragmented tech stack (Wix, WordPress, Segment) and lacks CRM, marketing automation, or analytics tools—creating significant operational bottlenecks. Our solution is well-positioned to address their core pain points: inefficient event lifecycle management, limited digital lead generation, and underutilized customer data. With a composite sell score of 0.61, moderate urgency (0.7), and strong strategic alignment (0.7), this is a viable sales opportunity requiring targeted outreach.
