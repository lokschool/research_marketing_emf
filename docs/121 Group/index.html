Executive Summary

121 Group operates a specialized matchmaking platform connecting downstream procurement and offtake teams with pre-vetted mining CEOs through private, double-opt-in 1-2-1 meetings. The company faces significant operational bottlenecks in manual scheduling, lead qualification, and cross-border coordination that limit scalability and create competitive vulnerability. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by automating scheduling workflows, enhancing lead scoring with mining-specific criteria, and providing integrated cross-border coordination tools—potentially reducing meeting setup time by 30-50% and increasing meeting capacity by 20-30% without additional headcount.
