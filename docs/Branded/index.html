Executive Summary

Branded is a niche event organizer operating across Asia's live sports, gaming, music, media, and entertainment sectors with a lean team of 47 employees. The company faces critical operational bottlenecks—fragmented data management, manual sponsor acquisition, and underleveraged attendee insights—that limit revenue growth and scalability. Our solution directly addresses these pain points by providing an integrated CRM and marketing automation platform that centralizes event data, automates lead scoring, and enables cross-event promotion. With a composite sell score of 0.71 and high urgency (0.9), Branded represents a strong near-term opportunity for a targeted sales engagement.

Company Overview

**Branded** is a Singapore-based event management company that convenes Asia's live sports, gaming, music, media, and entertainment sectors through a portfolio of major conferences:

  • **Live! Matters** – live events industry conference
  • **Music Matters** – music industry gathering
  • **Sports Matters** – sports business conference
  • **Gaming Matters** – esports and gaming industry event
  • **CreatorWeek** – content creator and influencer summit
