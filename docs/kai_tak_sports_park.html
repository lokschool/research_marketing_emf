Company Overview

Business Model

Kai Tak Sports Park (KTSP) is Hong Kong's newest and largest sports and entertainment venue complex (160 employees), built on the site of the former Kai Tak Airport. The HK$30 billion (USD ~$3.8B) government-backed development spans 28 hectares and includes a 50,000-seat main stadium, 10,000-seat indoor sports center, and 5,000-seat youth sports ground. Revenue from venue rental, event hosting, ticket sales, sponsorships, F&B, and commercial leasing. KTSP is a game-changing competitor to HKCEC and AsiaWorld-Expo for large-scale events.
