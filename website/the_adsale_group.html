Company Overview

Business Model

The Adsale Group is a Hong Kong-based trade exhibition organizer and industrial media company operating for decades across Asia-Pacific. It organizes B2B industrial trade shows, publishes trade magazines, and operates digital platforms. Its flagship is CHINAPLAS (International Plastics and Rubber Industries Exhibition), one of the world's largest plastics trade fairs. Revenue from exhibition space sales, sponsorship, advertising, conference registration, and digital media.
