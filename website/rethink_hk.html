Pain Point Analysis

#1: Pain Point 1: Lean Team Running Growing Conference

16 employees organizing Hong Kong's leading sustainability conference. Single flagship event annually. All revenue concentrated in one event.
Impact: Team stretched thin during event season. No bandwidth for year-round programming. Single point of failure for event execution. Cannot scale to additional events without hiring.
High — growth constrained by team size. Urgency

#2: Pain Point 2: No Year-Round Sustainability Community Platform

ReThink HK happens once annually. ESG/sustainability is a year-round business imperative — but the community only engages during conference days.
Impact: Massive missed opportunity for year-round content, networking, and community monetization. Attendees and sponsors get episodic value. Competitors (or media companies) could capture the HK sustainability community between events.
High — community ownership at risk. Urgency

#3: Pain Point 3: Manual Delegate & Sponsor Acquisition

16-person team likely doing manual outreach to corporate ESG leaders, government officials, NGOs, and green tech vendors.
Impact: Cannot scale attendee and sponsor acquisition without proportional headcount increase. Each new corporate sponsor requires manual sales cycles. No automated lead qualification.
Medium — growth bottleneck. Urgency

No evidence of automated follow-up, lead nurturing, or post-event attendee engagement.
